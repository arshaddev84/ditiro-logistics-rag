EXCEPTION HANDLING
1. Confirm the order ID and the most recent event timestamp.
2. Check the relevant source: order record, warehouse scan, driver update, or customer communication.
3. Distinguish confirmed facts from estimates, reports, and unverified alerts.
4. Escalate safety, security, temperature, damage, or delivery-window concerns to the responsible human operations team.
5. Do not change an order, promise a new ETA, or mark a shipment delivered based only on a model-generated answer.
6. Preserve the source and timestamp of information included in a customer update.

DOCUMENTATION AND PROOF OF DELIVERY
- A delivery is considered documented in this demo only when the order has a delivery event and the required proof-of-delivery evidence is attached.
- If a scan, signature, image, count, or seal detail is missing or inconsistent, flag it for human review.
- Do not infer a recipient's identity or signature from incomplete documents.
- Keep source document references attached to the shipment record when available.

COLD-CHAIN HANDLING
- Use the temperature range specified by the shipment's handling instruction; do not assume one range applies to every product.
- Record pre-trip checks and any available temperature readings against the shipment and vehicle.
- A temperature warning requires human review. Do not claim the goods remained within range unless supporting readings establish that.
- Do not recommend loading or continued transit when required conditions have not been verified.

SECURITY AND DRIVER SAFETY
- Treat crowd-sourced alerts and unverified reports as unconfirmed until checked through an approved operational source.
- Do not disclose a driver's personal details or precise live location to an unauthorized requester.
- Safety decisions remain with the driver and authorized operations personnel. The assistant may summarize available notices but must not direct a driver into or around a hazardous area.
- Report a security concern through the designated dispatch escalation channel.
